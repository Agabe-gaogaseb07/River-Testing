About Us

River to Sea Investment CC is a proudly Namibian-owned company specializing in the supply, installation, maintenance, and construction of water, wastewater, and fluid management infrastructure.


bcWe provide innovative, cost-effective, and sustainable solutions for municipalities, government institutions, mining companies, agricultural projects, industrial facilities, and residential clients throughout Namibia.

Vision

To become Namibia's leading provider of water, sewer, borehole, pipeline, and infrastructure solutions through excellence, innovation, and reliable service delivery.

Mission

To safely source, distribute, and store water across Namibia's communities and industrial sectors.
